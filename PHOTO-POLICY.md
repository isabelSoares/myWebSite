# Photo Usage Policy

All photographs on this site are © Isabel Soares. All rights reserved.

The photo albums are a personal visual notebook, not a stock library. Please do
not copy, republish, redistribute, sell, or use the photographs in other
projects without written permission. Linking to the public site is welcome.

## What is published

Only web-sized derivatives are published:

- thumbnails capped at 540 px and 120 KB;
- full-screen versions capped at 1800 px wide and 500 KB;
- WebP files with metadata removed.

The original photographs are kept in a private library outside this repository.
They are not committed, deployed, or linked from the site.

## Privacy and removal

If you appear in a photograph and want it removed, contact
[isabel.srsoares@gmail.com](mailto:isabel.srsoares@gmail.com) with the page and a
description of the image. The image will be unpublished and the derivatives
regenerated as soon as reasonably possible.

Removing a photo does not erase copies already downloaded, indexed, cached, or
present in earlier Git history. The project does not rewrite history for routine
photo removal.

## Permission requests

For permission to reuse a photograph, email
[isabel.srsoares@gmail.com](mailto:isabel.srsoares@gmail.com) with the image, the
intended use, and the expected audience.
